About EduPointAI

EduPointAI is an independent academic guidance platform designed to help students understand their academic qualifications and explore potential university courses.

Our Mission

To make academic information more accessible and help students make informed educational decisions using technology.

Our Vision

To become a trusted digital academic guidance platform that empowers students to discover educational opportunities and plan their future.

Who We Serve

  • KCSE candidates.
  • Students awaiting university placement.
  • Students exploring university courses.
  • Parents and guardians.
  • Students seeking alternative academic pathways.
Our Independence: EduPointAI is not KUCCPS, a university or a government admission authority. We provide guidance and calculations, not official admission decisions.

Rules & Regulations

These rules govern the general use of the EduPointAI platform.

Platform Rules: Users are expected to provide accurate information and use EduPointAI responsibly.

Account Registration

  • Users must provide accurate registration information.
  • Each user is responsible for maintaining the security of their account.
  • Users must not impersonate other people.

Academic Information

  • Students must submit accurate examination grades.
  • Students must not intentionally manipulate their academic records.
  • Results depend on the information submitted and the calculation methodology used.

Acceptable Use

Acceptable Use: Use the platform for legitimate academic guidance and educational purposes.

Users may calculate eligibility, explore courses, access purchased reports, and use academic guidance features.

Prohibited Activities: Unauthorized access, fraudulent activity, and system disruption are strictly prohibited.

Users must not attempt unauthorized access, interfere with functionality, exploit vulnerabilities, or submit fraudulent payment confirmations.
