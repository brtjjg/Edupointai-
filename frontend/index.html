About EduPointAI

Independent Academic Guidance

1. Who We Are

EduPointAI is an independent, technology-driven academic guidance platform developed to help students understand their academic qualifications, explore university courses and discover educational opportunities that align with their academic performance, interests and career aspirations.

Choosing a university course is one of the most important decisions in a student's educational journey. However, many students find it challenging to interpret their KCSE results, understand university course requirements, compare available programmes or identify alternative academic pathways.

EduPointAI seeks to make this process easier by bringing academic guidance, course exploration, qualification calculations and educational information together in one accessible digital platform.

Through technology, structured academic information and intelligent guidance tools, we help students make sense of their results and explore possibilities for their future.

Our goal is not simply to show students courses they may qualify for, but also to help them understand their options and make more informed educational decisions.

12. Our Promise

We believe every student deserves the opportunity to understand their academic potential and explore the possibilities available to them.
