About EduPointAI

Independent Academic Guidance

1. Who We Are

EduPointAI is an independent, technology-driven academic guidance platform developed to help students understand their academic qualifications, explore university courses and discover educational opportunities that align with their academic performance, interests and career aspirations.

Choosing a university course is one of the most important decisions in a student's educational journey. However, many students find it challenging to interpret their KCSE results, understand university course requirements, compare available programmes or identify alternative academic pathways.

EduPointAI seeks to make this process easier by bringing academic guidance, course exploration, qualification calculations and educational information together in one accessible digital platform.

Through technology, structured academic information and intelligent guidance tools, we help students make sense of their results and explore possibilities for their future.

Our goal is not simply to show students courses they may qualify for, but also to help them understand their options and make more informed educational decisions.

2. Our Mission

Our mission is to make academic information more accessible, understandable and useful to every student.

We aim to simplify the process of interpreting academic qualifications, exploring university programmes and identifying educational opportunities through reliable information and innovative technology.

We believe that students should have access to the information they need before making important decisions about their education.

3. Our Vision

To become a trusted digital academic guidance platform that empowers students to discover educational opportunities, understand their potential and confidently plan their academic and professional futures.

We envision a future where access to academic guidance is not limited by geographical location, financial background or access to traditional career counselling services.

4. Who We Serve

EduPointAI is designed for individuals at different stages of their educational journey.

🎓 KCSE Candidates

Students preparing for their future after completing secondary education can explore possible academic pathways and understand how their performance relates to university course requirements.

⏳ Students Awaiting Placement

Students waiting for placement can explore available courses, review historical admission requirements and prepare for their next academic steps.

🔍 Students Exploring Courses

Students who are uncertain about which programme to pursue can compare different courses, examine qualification requirements and discover alternative options.

👨‍👩‍👧 Parents and Guardians

Parents and guardians can use EduPointAI as an information resource to better understand their children's academic options and participate in educational planning.

5. What We Do

EduPointAI brings several academic guidance services together to simplify educational decision-making.

🧮 Academic Calculations

We help students interpret their academic results and calculate relevant qualification indicators to understand their academic standing.

🎓 University Course Exploration

Students can explore university programmes, review course requirements and identify programmes that may align with their qualifications.

🤖 AI-Powered Guidance

Our AI-assisted guidance tools help students explore career interests, understand academic pathways and discover related programmes.

💰 Scholarships & Opportunities

We aim to help students discover scholarship opportunities and other forms of educational support that may assist them in pursuing their studies.

6. Our Approach to Academic Guidance

We believe academic guidance should be understandable, accessible and practical. Our approach is built around five principles:

  • Accessibility: Making academic information easier to find and understand.
  • Clarity: Presenting academic calculations and course information in a straightforward manner.
  • Technology: Using digital tools and AI-assisted systems to improve the guidance experience.
  • Informed decisions: Helping students understand their options rather than making educational decisions on their behalf.
  • Continuous improvement: Improving our platform, information resources and tools as educational requirements and student needs evolve.

7. Our Commitment to Students

Every student's academic journey is different. A student's KCSE performance should be a starting point for exploring opportunities, not a reason to stop pursuing their ambitions.

At EduPointAI, we are committed to helping students:

  • Understand their academic qualifications.
  • Explore programmes beyond their initial choices.
  • Identify potential course alternatives.
  • Discover educational and scholarship opportunities.
  • Make decisions based on available academic information.
  • Approach their educational future with greater confidence.

We aim to make academic guidance more approachable, particularly for students who may not have access to personalised educational counselling.

8. Our Independence and Transparency

An Independent Academic Guidance Platform

EduPointAI operates independently. It is not affiliated with, owned by, operated by or officially endorsed by the Kenya Universities and Colleges Central Placement Service (KUCCPS), any university, the Ministry of Education or any government admission authority.

Our platform provides academic guidance, calculations, educational information and course exploration tools.

Our calculations and course comparisons are intended for informational and guidance purposes. They do not constitute official admission decisions, placement confirmations or guarantees of university admission.

Official admission, placement, programme availability and eligibility decisions remain the responsibility of the relevant authorised institutions.

We encourage students to verify important admission information, programme requirements, application deadlines and placement decisions through the appropriate official channels before taking action.
